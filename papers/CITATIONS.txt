Reference PDFs for architecture and training.
Read these before changing the goal-conditioned trainer.

uvfa-schaul-2015.pdf
  Schaul, Horgan, Gregor, Silver.
  Universal Value Function Approximators.
  ICML 2015.
  https://proceedings.mlr.press/v37/schaul15.html
  Why: one policy V(s, g) / pi(a | s, g) over many equilibria.

her-andrychowicz-2017.pdf
  Andrychowicz et al.
  Hindsight Experience Replay.
  NeurIPS 2017. arXiv:1707.01495
  Why: sparse "did we reach this equilibrium?" rewards become usable
  by relabeling failed rollouts with the goal we actually landed in.

spong-acrobot-swingup-1995.pdf
  Spong, M. W.
  The Swing Up Control Problem for the Acrobot.
  IEEE Control Systems Magazine, 1995.
  Why: energy-based swing-up + local capture. The hybrid template
  behind UU/UD/DU/DD transitions.

energy-swingup-dip-ifac-2008.pdf
  IFAC 2008 analysis of energy-based swing-up for a double pendulum
  on a cart (cites Zhong & Rock, IEEE CCA 2001).
  https://skoge.folk.ntnu.no/prost/proceedings/ifac2008/data/papers/2922.pdf
  Why: energy landscape and multiple equilibria of this exact plant.

deep-rl-continuous-control-2016.pdf
  Duan, Chen, Houthooft, Schulman, Abbeel.
  Benchmarking Deep Reinforcement Learning for Continuous Control.
  arXiv:1604.06778
  Why: DDPG / continuous-control baselines; double inverted pendulum
  as a benchmark task.

inverted-double-pendulum-rl-gustafsson-2016.pdf
  Gustafsson.
  Control of Inverted Double Pendulum using Reinforcement Learning.
  Stanford CS229, 2016.
  Why: practical RL on inverted double pendulum; notes on swing-up
  vs local balance.

mb-rl-double-pendulum-2024.pdf
  Learning control of underactuated double pendulum with
  Model-Based Reinforcement Learning.
  arXiv:2409.05811
  Why: recent model-based RL on an underactuated double pendulum.
